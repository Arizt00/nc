# High Level Final Surface

Status: closed by High Level Fase 16.

High-level is the umbrella for every nC surface that is not basal [@], primitive[@], [@machine], [@system], or [@kernel]. This includes normal nC, [@runtime], [@compiler], [@inspect], [@ai], basic high-level, advanced high-level and expert high-level.

Fase 16 adds no feature semantics. It seals the index, doctor and manifest surface after Fases 1-15 closed parser semantics, type system, generics, traits, dispatch, ADTs, error algebra, iterators, async/effects, modules, closures, reflection, compile-time evaluation, macros and risk authority.

Runtime and compiler remain official CLI surfaces. The final high-level doctor reflects their status and prepares the ordered unification path.
